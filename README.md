# simple-interest-calculator
A simple interest calculator

This project explains how to calculate simple interest.

## Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

- **Principal:** The initial amount of money invested or borrowed.
- **Rate:** The annual interest rate (in percentage).
- **Time:** The duration in years.

## Example

If you invest ₦10,000 at an annual interest rate of 5% for 2 years:

Simple Interest = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = ₦1,000

## Result

The simple interest earned is ₦1,000.

The total amount is ₦11,000.
